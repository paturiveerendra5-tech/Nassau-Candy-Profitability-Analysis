# Executive Summary

## Performance

The provided file contains 10,194 transaction rows for 15 products. Recorded sales total **141,783.63**, cost **48,340.83**, gross profit **93,442.80**, and weighted gross margin **65.91%**. Currency is unspecified. Gross profit reconciles to sales less cost for all rows.

## What drives profit and where is risk?

- **Top profit SKU:** Wonka Bar -Scrumdiddlyumptious — 19,357.50 gross profit (20.72% share).
- **Division:** Chocolate provides 95.06% of profit at 67.45% margin. Other has the lowest divisional margin (44.84%).
- **Concentration:** 5 of 15 products (33.3%) produce 95.06% of gross profit.
- **High sales / low margin:** Kazookles.
- **Lowest product margins:** Kazookles (7.69%), Fun Dip (40.00%), Nerds (46.67%).

## Management actions to consider

1. Review price/discount and cost detail for high-sales, low-margin products before repricing.
2. Diagnose product mix and cost/price drivers in Other; do not assume the cause from this dataset.
3. Monitor exposure to the small group of products producing most gross profit.
4. Benchmark high absolute product costs against approved cost standards before targeting savings.
5. Verify the anomalous order/ship dates with the source owner.
6. Supply an authoritative product-to-factory mapping before judging factory performance.

## Caveats

All 10,194 order-to-ship gaps exceed one year (range 904–1642 days). No rows were deleted or dates corrected. Factory profitability is unavailable because the product-to-factory mapping is missing. Geographic findings are descriptive and support no logistics claims.
